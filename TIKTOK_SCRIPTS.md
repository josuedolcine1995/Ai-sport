**Rules:** Film on your phone, vertical. Don't aim for perfect — aim for POSTED. One per day. You don't need your face for most of these: screen-record ChatGPT/Claude or film your hands/screen. Put **trylaunchstack.lemonsqueezy.com** in your bio TODAY (bio link needs no follower minimum — "link in bio" in captions still works because people tap your profile).

**Caption formula for every video:** hook line + 2-3 hashtags max (#smallbusiness #freelancer #sidehustle #chatgpt — rotate). Never say "buy my product" in the video; say "the full vault/kit is in my bio."
