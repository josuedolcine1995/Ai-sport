# 🤖 The AI Prompt Vault for Small Business

### 100+ copy-paste prompts for ChatGPT & Claude — organized by what you're trying to get done.

**How to use this vault:** Find your category, copy the prompt, replace anything in `[BRACKETS]` with your details, and paste it into ChatGPT or Claude. Pro tip: after the first answer, reply with *"Make it punchier"* or *"Give me 3 more variations"* — the second draft is almost always better.

7. Act as a brand strategist. My business is [DESCRIBE]. Give me a brand voice guide: 5 adjectives, 3 do's, 3 don'ts, and 2 example sentences written in that voice.

9. Map the full customer journey for someone buying [PRODUCT] — from first hearing about me to repeat purchase — and flag the 2 weakest points in my current funnel: [DESCRIBE CURRENT FUNNEL].
10. Give me 7 promotion ideas for [HOLIDAY/SEASON] for a [TYPE OF BUSINESS], each with a hook, an offer, and a deadline mechanic.

## ⭐ Bonus: 5 Power Techniques

101. **The Critic Pass** — after any draft: "Now critique this like a skeptical customer. List what's weak, then rewrite it fixing those weaknesses."
102. **The Style Clone** — "Here are 3 examples of my writing: [PASTE]. Learn my voice, then write [NEW THING] exactly in that voice."
103. **The Interview Mode** — "Don't write yet. Ask me 5 questions one at a time to get what you need, THEN write [THING]."
104. **The Simplifier** — "Explain/rewrite this so a smart 12-year-old gets it instantly: [PASTE]."
105. **The Options Fan** — "Give me 5 wildly different approaches to [TASK] — safe, bold, cheap, premium, and weird. One paragraph each."
