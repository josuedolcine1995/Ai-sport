# 🚀 Client Onboarding Checklist

> A smooth first week is why clients trust you, pay on time, and refer their friends. Run this checklist every time you close a new client. (Items marked ⚡ have a ready-to-send message below.)

## Day 0 — The moment they say yes
- [ ] ⚡ Send the welcome email (template A below)
- [ ] Send contract for signature
- [ ] Send deposit invoice
- [ ] Create a project folder (Drive/Dropbox) and share it with the client

## Day 1 — Money cleared, make it official
- [ ] Confirm deposit received — ⚡ send confirmation + what happens next (template B)
- [ ] Send the intake questionnaire (template C)
- [ ] Book the kickoff call
- [ ] Add all project milestones to your calendar with reminders

## Kickoff call agenda (30 min)
- [ ] 5 min — rapport + confirm their #1 goal in their own words
- [ ] 10 min — walk through timeline and what you need from them (and when)
- [ ] 10 min — go through intake answers, fill gaps
- [ ] 5 min — agree on communication: where (email/Slack), how often (weekly update), who approves things

## During the project — every single week
- [ ] ⚡ Send a Friday progress update even if nothing is due (template D) — this one habit eliminates 90% of client anxiety
- [ ] Log any out-of-scope requests immediately and quote them separately (never silently absorb scope creep)

## Delivery day
- [ ] Deliver final work + a short "how to use it" note or video
- [ ] Send final invoice
- [ ] ⚡ 3 days later: ask for a testimonial + referral (template E)

## Copy-paste templates

**A — Welcome:**
> Hi [NAME] — thrilled to be working together! Two quick things to get us moving: (1) the agreement is on its way to your inbox for signature, and (2) the deposit invoice is attached. Once both are done, I'll send over a short questionnaire and we'll book the kickoff. Excited to get you [THEIR GOAL]!

**B — Deposit confirmed:**
> Deposit received — thank you, [NAME]! You're officially on my calendar. Next step: the questionnaire below (10 minutes, helps me nail this). Then we kick off on [DATE].

**C — Intake questionnaire (adapt to your service):**
> 1. In one sentence, what does a home-run result look like for this project?
> 2. Who exactly is the end customer/audience?
> 3. What have you tried before, and what didn't work about it?
> 4. Any examples you love (or hate)? Links welcome.
> 5. Anything that would make this project a failure even if everything else goes right?
> 6. Who besides you needs to approve the final result?

**D — Friday update:**
> Hi [NAME] — weekly update: ✅ Done: [X]. 🔨 In progress: [Y]. 📅 Next week: [Z]. ⚠️ Waiting on you: [ITEM or "nothing right now"]. On track for [DATE].

**E — Testimonial + referral ask:**
> Hi [NAME] — now that [PROJECT] is wrapped, two tiny favors: (1) Could you reply with 2-3 sentences about the experience/results? I'd love to feature it. (2) If you know one person who needs [WHAT YOU DO], an intro would mean the world. Either way — it was a pleasure!
